01. Records of one person and their countries
Check: combine every record sharing a phone and full date of birth
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / LOCATION_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the two records of one person are linked to the United Kingdom and Canada, neither of which is the hit's Australia, and the hit has no date of birth for the exception.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730011, CUS-730012, H-F00301#countries

02. Latest approved check
Check: verified facts beat what the customer declared
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the latest approved check gives a date of birth in 1960, which outranks the older check and the declared date; the hit's 1984 contradicts it.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730021, KYC-C00302, H-F00302#dobs

03. Rejected check
Check: a rejected result must not replace a valid identity
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the newer rejected check is not evidence; the valid date of birth is still 1984, which contradicts the hit's 1960.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730031, KYC-D03031, H-F00303#dobs

04. Onboarding check note
Check: a valid note can give the latest verified facts
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the onboarding note records an approved check newer than the table's, with a date of birth in 1960, which contradicts the hit's 1984.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730041, NOTE-SYN-3-04, H-F00304#dobs

05. Joint account
Check: pick the right holder from the screened details
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: in the joint account the screened name and date of birth fit the original customer (1984); the other holder must not be used to match the hit.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730051, KYC-D03051, H-F00305#dobs

06. Country from another record
Check: a shared country appears after merging
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the same phone and full date of birth prove one person; the second record adds a Canada link, and name, date of birth and country agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730061, CUS-730062, H-F00306#countries, H-F00306#dobs

07. Same document number
Check: different phones can still be one person
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the approved checks of both records use the same document number, so they merge and keep the Canada link.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730071, CUS-730072, KYC-D03071, KYC-D03072, H-F00307#countries

08. Check note precedence
Check: an approved check in a note can correct a declared date of birth
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the expired check is not used; the latest valid onboarding check note gives a date of birth in 1984, which agrees with the hit.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730081, NOTE-SYN-3-08, H-F00308#dobs

09. No direct link
Check: search for the customer by the screened details
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the alert has no account, login or email; the screened name, date of birth, country and city find exactly one customer, whose identity facts agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730091, KYC-D03091, H-F00309#dobs

10. Family sharing a phone
Check: same phone, different date of birth must not merge
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the customer (1984) only shares a phone with another customer (1985), so they do not merge; the hit's 1985 is one year from the customer's, which needs review.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730101, KYC-D030A1, H-F0030A#dobs

11. Shared address
Check: the same address does not prove the same person
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: a shared address does not prove one person, so the other record's 1985 date of birth cannot be used to confirm; the customer's 1984 is one year from the hit's, which needs review.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730111, KYC-D030B1, H-F0030B#dobs

12. Several candidate customers
Check: different candidates lead to different decisions
Expected: REVIEW / AMBIGUOUS_CUSTOMER_RECORD
Policy basis: two different customers under the same email both fit the screening; the 1984 candidate matches and the 1960 candidate does not, so the hit is ambiguous and needs review.
Deciding evidence: CUS-730121, CUS-730122

13. Customer not found
Check: do not guess an identity when no customer is found
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the screened details find no customer, and another customer's records must not be used.
Deciding evidence: A-E0030D
