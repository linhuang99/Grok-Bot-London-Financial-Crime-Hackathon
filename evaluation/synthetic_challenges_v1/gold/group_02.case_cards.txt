01. Age at a date
Check: keep both possible birth years
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the age expression gives 1959 or 1960, both more than 1 year from the customer's 1984.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720011, KYC-D02011, H-F00201#profile_notes

02. Under 16
Check: the earliest report date in the policy's exact format
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / TEMPORAL_IMPOSSIBILITY
Policy basis: the customer was only 8 at the 2018-01-01 report, below the policy's 16-year threshold.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720021, KYC-D02021, H-F00202#profile_notes

03. Company incorporation
Check: incorporated after the earliest report
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / TEMPORAL_IMPOSSIBILITY
Policy basis: the company was incorporated only in 2022, so it cannot be the company in a 2019 report.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720031, KYC-D02031, H-F00203#profile_notes

04. Partial date of death
Check: use the date range, never invent a month or day
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DECEASED
Policy basis: even taking the last day of 2019, the death is before the 2026 screening date.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720041, H-F00204#deceased_date, A-E00204

05. Same year and month, different day
Check: a clear contradiction in the date of birth's detail
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: both give the same year and month, but the days are 14 and 15, a clear contradiction.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720051, KYC-D02051, H-F00205#dobs

06. Year only
Check: a partial date can agree with a full date
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the hit's year is compatible with the customer's full date of birth, and name and country agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720061, KYC-D02061, H-F00206#dobs

07. Date of birth in the profile
Check: read a stated date of birth from the first paragraph
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the date of birth stated in the first paragraph agrees with the customer's; name and country agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720071, KYC-D02071, H-F00207#profile_notes

08. Age in a search result
Check: pick the right person first, then work out the year
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: in the qualifying search result the person is 27, so born in 1984 or 1985; 1984 agrees with the customer.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720081, KYC-D02081, SR-SYN-2-08-1

09. Several candidate dates of birth
Check: one agreeing date prevents a date-of-birth clear
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the hit allows several dates of birth and at least one agrees with the customer's full date; name and country agree with no separate contradiction.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720091, KYC-D02091, H-F00209#dobs

10. A witness's age
Check: never give another person's age to the listed person
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the age 25 belongs to a witness, not the listed person. The listed person's date of birth is unknown; name and country agree but do not settle it.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720101, SR-SYN-2-10-1

11. Several people in one sentence
Check: do not force an age onto the listed person
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the two ages belong to a witness and a lawyer; the listed person's date of birth is still unknown.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720111, SR-SYN-2-11-1

12. Negative sentence
Check: "no date of birth given" is not a date of birth
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the profile says the listed person's age or date of birth is not given; a date must not be invented from keywords.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720121, KYC-D020C1, H-F0020C#dobs

13. Age threshold boundary
Check: exactly 16 is not under 16
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the customer was exactly 16 on the report date, so the hit cannot be cleared as under 16; the hit has no date of birth, so it cannot be confirmed either.
Deciding evidence: CUS-720131, KYC-D020D1, H-F0020D#profile_notes
