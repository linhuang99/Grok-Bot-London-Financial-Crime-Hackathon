01. Category distractor
Check: ignore a namesake in the same country but a different category
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the musician's 1984 must not be used; the listed person matching the category and country was born in 1960, which contradicts the customer.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740011, KYC-D04011, SR-SYN-4-01-2

02. Country distractor
Check: ignore a same-category namesake in another country
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the hit is linked only to the United Kingdom, so the Nigerian namesake is not used; the valid UK result gives a date of birth in 1960, a contradiction.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740021, KYC-D04021, SR-SYN-4-02-2

03. Alias and date of birth
Check: an agreeing name does not cancel a separate date-of-birth contradiction
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / DOB_MISMATCH
Policy basis: an alias agrees with the customer's name, but every date of birth on the hit is 1960, which clearly contradicts the customer's 1984.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740031, KYC-D04031, H-F00403#dobs

04. Placeholder alias
Check: a placeholder must not create name agreement
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / NAME_MISMATCH_VERIFIED
Policy basis: the placeholder alias is not a real name, and the customer's verified name does not agree with the hit's real names.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740041, KYC-D04041, H-F00404#name, H-F00404#aliases

05. A place outside the address fields
Check: the nationality field can also hold a city and country
Expected: DIFFERENT_ENTITY / LOCATION_MISMATCH
Policy basis: the place in the nationality field clearly gives Canada; the customer is linked only to the United Kingdom, and the hit has no agreeing date of birth for the exception.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740051, KYC-D04051, H-F00405#nationality

06. Agreeing alias
Check: a different main name must not hide a valid alias
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: the main name differs, but a valid alias agrees with the customer's verified name, and date of birth and country agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740061, KYC-D04061, H-F00406#dobs

07. Accents and order
Check: accents and order must not create a name contradiction
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: ignoring accents and case and comparing name parts, the names agree; date of birth and country agree.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740071, KYC-D04071, H-F00407#dobs

08. Company name variant
Check: the substantive company name parts agree
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: ignoring the legal form and the order of name parts, the substantive name and the country agree, with no separate contradiction.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740081, KYC-D04081, H-F00408#dobs

09. Territory and code
Check: keep territories separate and recognise codes
Expected: SAME_ENTITY / NAME_DOB_LOCATION_MATCH
Policy basis: HK and Hong Kong are the same territory; the customer's and the hit's names and dates of birth agree, and Hong Kong must not be mapped to mainland China.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740091, KYC-D04091, H-F00409#dobs

10. Unrecognised country
Check: a partly unreadable country set must not be silently trimmed
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: one country value cannot be reliably recognised and may affect the overlap; with no date of birth, it cannot be shown that every country differs, nor can identity be confirmed.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740101, KYC-D040A1, H-F0040A#dobs

11. Invalid date of birth
Check: an impossible calendar date is not grounds for a contradiction
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: 31 February is not a valid date and counts as unusable information, not a date-of-birth contradiction; the other facts do not settle it.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740111, KYC-D040B1, H-F0040B#dobs

12. Several adjacent years
Check: no date agrees and none is a hard contradiction
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: both possible years are 1 year from 1984; no year agrees and none is more than 1 year apart, so it needs review.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740121, KYC-D040C1, H-F0040C#dobs

13. Exception and an unrelated search
Check: structured dates come first, and the country exception still applies
Expected: REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT_DATA
Policy basis: the structured name and date of birth agree with the customer but the countries differ, so it must be reviewed; a musician in another country must not override the hit's structured date of birth.
Deciding evidence: CUS-740131, KYC-D040D1, H-F0040D#dobs
